Browser ladder bench

Captures a tab or screen, paces it onto a fixed grid (duplicating the last frame when the source goes quiet, which is what a static tab does), fans one paced frame out to three renditions sharing an identical PTS and one keyframe decision, encodes audio as AAC, and publishes the whole ladder to the relay as a moq-lite broadcast (catalog.json + one legacy track per rendition). HLS is served by the origin at /<name>/index.m3u8.
