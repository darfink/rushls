Browser → HLS (MoQ ingest) test

Captures with the browser's own APIs (Camera+Mic = getUserMedia, Screen = getDisplayMedia), encodes with WebCodecs, and publishes to the origin over WebTransport (moq-lite-05) as a hang legacy broadcast with an H.264 avc3 video track and an Opus audio track.

The origin is http:// on purpose. Chromium will not complete a WebTransport handshake against a certificate signed by a locally installed root, so the client fetches /certificate.sha256 from that origin, pins it with serverCertificateHashes, and connects over QUIC on the same port — TCP and UDP have separate port spaces, so one number serves both. Mint the certificate with tools/mint-dev-cert.sh. A publicly trusted certificate needs none of this: use https:// and the fingerprint is ignored.

Playable about 6 seconds after the origin reports tracks discovered.
