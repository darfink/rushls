Browser → HLS (MoQ ingest) test

Captures with the browser's own APIs (Camera+Mic = getUserMedia, Screen = getDisplayMedia), encodes with WebCodecs, and publishes to the origin over WebTransport (moq-lite-05) as a hang legacy broadcast with an H.264 avc3 video track, an Opus audio track, and optionally a utf8 caption track that the origin packages as WebVTT subtitles.

A caption is stamped with performance.now() in microseconds when you send it, the same clock the library stamps captured video and audio with, so it lands on the frame that was on screen at that moment. It stays up until the next caption replaces it or Clear removes it.

The origin is http:// on purpose. Chromium will not complete a WebTransport handshake against a certificate signed by a locally installed root, so the client fetches /certificate.sha256 from that origin, pins it with serverCertificateHashes, and connects over QUIC on the same port — TCP and UDP have separate port spaces, so one number serves both. Mint the certificate with tools/mint-dev-cert.sh. A publicly trusted certificate needs none of this: use https:// and the fingerprint is ignored.

Playable about 6 seconds after the origin reports tracks discovered.
